Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Welcome to TipsyRoaming. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy outlines how we handle the information collected when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to ensure the functionality and security of the site:

  • User-Generated Content: When you publish a review in our Study, Sightseeing, or Gourmet sections, the text content, selected language, and timestamp are securely stored via Google Firebase Realtime Database.
  • Anonymous Authentication: We use Firebase Anonymous Authentication to assign a temporary, invisible ID to your session to prevent spam. This does not collect your name, email, or personal identity.
  • Usage Data: We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with our site. This includes anonymous data such as pages visited, clicks, scrolling behavior, and general geographic location.

2. How We Use Your Information

The collected data is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To display your shared reviews and travel tips to other users.
  • To improve website performance, user experience, and content quality.
  • To maintain the security of our platform and prevent malicious bot activities.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use standard cookies provided by our third-party analytics partners (Google and Microsoft) to track site traffic. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings without affecting your ability to browse the site.

4. Data Security

We implement strict security rules using Firebase's backend validation to ensure data integrity. However, please remember that any reviews you publish are publicly visible. Do not share sensitive personal information in the review sections.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request the removal of a specific review you posted, please connect with us using the Contact section on our homepage.
